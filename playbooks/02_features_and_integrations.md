# SAMPLE PLAYBOOK - PRODUCT CONVERSATION
This is illustrative material, not a source of product promises or technical SLAs.

## Inquiry and follow-up management
For inquiries that get lost, ask where incoming leads originate, how reps are assigned, how follow-up dates are set, and how overdue work is reviewed. Demonstrate only actual product screens the company has verified.

## Offers, invoices, and dispatch
If the buyer asks about the transition from quotation to order and dispatch, ask who prepares offers, who approves orders, how invoices are generated, and how dispatch status reaches the sales team. Confirm the customer's required approval chain before showing workflow features.

## Integration requests
If the customer wants to connect another system, ask for the system name, data objects, direction of sync, event frequency, authentication model, and the business owner. Do not promise an integration is available without technical validation.

## Privacy and data handling
Never request passwords or unnecessary customer information on the call. Do not claim specific security certifications, storage locations, or retention terms without approved documentation.
