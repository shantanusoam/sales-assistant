# SAMPLE PLAYBOOK - DISCOVERY AND OBJECTIONS
Replace this example document with your company's approved playbooks before a real pilot.

## Spreadsheet and WhatsApp objection
When a prospect says the team already uses Excel, spreadsheets, or WhatsApp, ask which process is hardest to track. Explore the handoffs between capturing an inquiry, assigning an owner, scheduling a follow-up, sending an offer, and confirming an order. Do not claim the prospect needs replacement of every existing tool.

## Pricing objection
When the customer says the system is too expensive, don't invent a quote, savings figure, discount, or ROI guarantee. Ask how many enquiries go unattended, how much time the team spends compiling updates, and what scope would be valuable first. Share actual pricing only from an approved current quote.

## Implementation concern
When the customer is concerned about setup and training, ask which team will use the product first, which single workflow matters most, and who owns the data preparation. Agree on a small, testable launch milestone instead of promising instant adoption.

## Next step
Close a discovery call by confirming the top problem, the stakeholder who approves the decision, and one specific follow-up action with an owner and date.
